01

Research focus

My work sits at the intersection of field phenotyping and genomics — identifying which wheat genotypes stay productive under stress, and why, so that breeding programs can move faster from trial data to recommended varieties.

  • Stress phenotyping

    Evaluating wheat genotypes for tolerance to heat, drought, and rust across multi-environment trials.

  • Marker-assisted genomics

    SSR marker analysis and QTL/GWAS approaches linked to stress-tolerance traits.

  • Genotype stability analysis

    AMMI and related models for genotype-by-environment interaction, used to identify genotypes that perform reliably across contrasting moisture regimes.

  • Climate-resilient breeding & food security

    Connecting trial results to breeding decisions that support food security across South Asia.
