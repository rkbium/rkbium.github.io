Research focus

I want to help meet the challenge of global food and nutritional security through plant breeding and genetics — developing crop varieties that withstand extreme climate, pests, and diseases using both molecular and field-based tools, and that work for farmers in rural and urban agriculture alike.

  • Abiotic stress breeding in wheat

    Evaluating wheat genotypes under irrigated, rainfed, drought, heat, and combined heat–drought environments to identify high-yielding, stress-tolerant lines.

  • G × E interaction & stability

    AMMI and GGE biplot models, stress tolerance indices, and selection parameters to find genotypes that perform reliably across contrasting environments.

  • Molecular breeding

    SSR marker characterisation for heat, drought, and rust resistance; gene expression under individual and combined heat and drought stress; genetic diversity and population structure.

  • Biotic stress & crop physiology

    Spot blotch and powdery mildew severity across growing environments, crop growth rates, root architecture, and the contribution of plant parts to grain yield.
